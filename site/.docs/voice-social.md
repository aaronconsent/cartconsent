# Social & Ad Voice — Consent Resolve (Heartbeat v2)

> **Scope:** This canon governs **social posts and ad/video scripts ONLY.** The
> website keeps its warm-inbound voice (`.docs/voice.md`) and its named-competitor
> comparison pages. Do not apply these rules site-wide.

## Who's talking
A contractor peer who figured something out — **never a spokesperson, never a
marketer.** First line sounds **spoken mid-thought**, not written. Plausibility
beats persuasion.

## Structure
One **pain** → one **flip** → soft CTA (**consentresolve.com/demo**). 12–30s for
video; 1–3 short sentences for a post.

## Tone calibration
Dry, a little tired, numerically precise, occasionally darkly funny — *the humor
of a guy reading his own invoice out loud.* Never hype. **No exclamation points.**

## Hard rules
1. **Never name competitors.** Use: "the big lead sites," "the platform fined
   $7.2 million," "those visitor-ID tools," "Local Service Ads."
2. **Never claim a specific % of visitors identified.** ("98 of 100 *leave*
   anonymous" — the leak — is allowed; "we identify X%" is not.)
3. **No phone numbers, no cold-calling, no gray-area data.** We deliver a real
   name, a real email, and what they were looking for.
4. **No trial offers or trial pricing of any kind.**
5. Pull pains/numbers/phrasing only from the banks below — do not invent stats.

## Use
exclusive · never resold · your own traffic · your own website · real people ·
consent-first · no contracts · no phone numbers · $7 a lead · recover · already
paid for · your funnel · your brand · receipts · the math

## Avoid
trial/trial pricing · revolutionary · AI-powered · game-changer · solution ·
leverage · unlock · spoken competitor brand names · any "% of visitors
identified" claim · phone/SMS-outreach implications · "tracking people around
the internet" · exclamation points

## Pain banks (ad-safe)
- **Shared/recycled leads:** same homeowner sold to 3–10 contractors; a race to
  call, then a price war. "They win, you lose."
- **Fake/phantom leads:** charged $30–$170 for homeowners who never asked;
  contacts unreachable 75%+ of the time; "4 jobs from 54 leads."
- **Billing abuse:** charged the instant a lead arrives; refunds as credit, not
  money; 12-month contract pressure.
- **LSA:** a robot reviews/charges with no manual dispute (removed mid-2024),
  credits up to 30 days, no appeal — "charged ~$400 for a call from an existing
  client." Your pipeline lives in someone else's dashboard.
- **Visitor-ID incumbents:** gray-area/implicit consent, "email laundering,"
  "why am I on this list?" emails, high spam-complaint rates, inflated dashboards,
  $500/mo minimums.

## Receipts (verifiable)
- FTC final order, April 2023: the big lead company ordered to pay up to **$7.2M**
  for deceptive lead-selling claims (FTC Matter 1923106).
- ~98 of 100 website visitors leave anonymous.

## Flips (our counters)
- gray-area consent → **consent-first**, defensible identification
- "email laundering" → trusted, deterministic data from **your own traffic**
- TCPA exposure → **no phone numbers, ever**
- $500/mo minimums → **$7 per lead** — pay for leads, not a platform
- inflated dashboards → **you only pay for real, identified leads; the invoice is
  the attribution**
- shared leads → **exclusive, sold once, to you, never resold**

## Hook angles (first lines must sound spoken)
1. Invoice read · 2. The race · 3. FTC $7.2M receipt · 4. The 98% leak ·
5. Same lead twice · 6. The ghost · 7. Credit game · 8. Robot billing (LSA) ·
9. One policy change · 10. Creepy-tool contrast · 11. Math flip · 12. Ownership ·
13. Contrarian ("lead sites work exactly as designed — for them").

## CTA
Soft, single: **consentresolve.com/demo**. For resource-share posts the link
stays the resource; the demo is a soft secondary mention.
