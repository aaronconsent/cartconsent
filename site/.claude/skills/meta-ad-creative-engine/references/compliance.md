# Compliance — the never-say list

> **The style guide is the source of truth for brand + voice.** Read the voice canon
> (`src/pages/style-guide/voice.astro`, live `/style-guide/voice/`) before writing copy — this
> file is the *ads-specific compliance layer* on top of it, not a replacement. The voice/banned-word
> section below mirrors the style guide; if they ever differ, the style guide wins and you should
> flag the drift so it gets reconciled. Run drafts through the repo's `voice-check` skill to enforce.


Consent Resolve's entire market position is **consent-first**. Two audiences police this: Meta's
ad review (which rejects ads that imply covert surveillance or make outcome guarantees) and our
own brand promise (which falls apart the moment a creative sounds like a data broker). A hook
that violates these isn't "edgy" — it undoes the thing we sell. Treat this as a hard filter that
runs *before* creativity, then write the sharpest compliant line you can.

## Never claim, imply, or illustrate

1. **Phone-number delivery.** We deliver **name + email + intent**, and follow-up is by email.
   Never say we hand over phone numbers, never imply the contractor can call the visitor, never
   show a phone/dialer as the payoff. This is a TCPA-safety line, not a style preference —
   phone-based claims invite exactly the liability we position against. If a hook needs a "reach
   them" beat, it's "email them back", "follow up", "reply".

2. **Covert tracking / "spying" language.** No "spy on your visitors", "secret", "stealth",
   "we track everyone who lands", "unmask anonymous visitors", "de-anonymize", "fingerprint",
   "follow them around the web". The visitor **opts in** — the story is *consent*, identity the
   visitor *agreed* to share. Say "visitors who raise their hand", "opt-in", "consent-first".

3. **Income / results guarantees.** No "double your bookings", "guaranteed jobs", "you'll close
   X", "$50k more this month", specific ROI promises framed as certainties. Meta's ad policy
   bans unrealistic outcome claims, and we can't guarantee a contractor's close rate. Use *their*
   math, framed as illustration: "a booked HVAC job is worth ~$X — one lead pays for a lot of
   these" with the contractor supplying the numbers, not a promise.

4. **Personal-attribute call-outs (Meta Special Category risk).** Don't address the viewer by an
   implied protected/sensitive attribute. We're B2B to contractors — keep it about the business
   ("your shop", "your site", "your trade"), never assumptions about the person.

5. **Fear-mongering about "being watched" or legal doom.** We can note that shared-lead resellers
   and shady tracking are a problem, but the tone is confident and matter-of-fact, not "you're
   about to get sued". Contrast with competitors on *value and exclusivity*, not scare tactics.

6. **Naming the visitor-ID vendors or "how" in a creepy way.** Don't get into pixels,
   fingerprinting, match rates, or third-party data in the ad. The ad sells the *outcome*
   (exclusive, consent-first leads), not the plumbing.

## Say instead (swap table)

| If you're tempted to write… | Write instead |
|---|---|
| "get their phone number" | "get their name, email, and what they need" |
| "call the leads we find" | "follow up by email while it's hot" |
| "we track your anonymous visitors" | "we hand back the visitors who opt in" |
| "spy on / unmask your traffic" | "turn opt-in visitors into exclusive leads" |
| "guaranteed to double your jobs" | "one booked job is worth ~$X — do the math" |
| "free leads" | "your first leads" / "$7 each" (never the word *free*; brand voice bans it) |
| "instant" | "fast", "same day", "while it's hot" (brand voice bans *instant*) |

## Positive framing that's always safe

- Exclusivity: "yours alone, never resold" (vs shared-lead resellers who sell the same lead to
  4–5 contractors).
- Consent-first: "nobody's identified until they say yes."
- Ownership: "your traffic, your leads, your list — not rented from a platform."
- Price honesty: "$7 a lead, flat. No contract."
- The waste stat: "~98% of the people who land on your site leave without a trace" — this is
  about *your own site's* bounce, not covert tracking, so it's clean. Keep it framed as *your
  traffic you're already paying for*, never as us watching strangers.

## Voice guardrails (from the site style guide)

Banned words in copy: **free, instant, seamless, supercharge, world-class, game-changer,
revolutionize, best-in-class, cutting-edge, unlock, effortless**. Prefer "no charge" over "free".
Plain-spoken and a little irreverent beats hype every time — contractors have a finely tuned
BS detector.

**Final gate:** before emitting any batch, re-read each primary text / headline / description
and each image concept against items 1–6. If a line trips one, rewrite it — don't ship it with a
caveat.
