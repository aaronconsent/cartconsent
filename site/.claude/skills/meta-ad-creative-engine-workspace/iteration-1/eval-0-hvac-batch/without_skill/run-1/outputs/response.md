## Positioning notes (read before shipping)

- **Buyer = the HVAC contractor**, not the homeowner. Every concept speaks contractor-to-contractor: "your website," "your leads," "your service area." Do not accidentally write copy that sounds like it's selling AC repair to homeowners.
- **The hook is exclusivity + consent + price.** Three differentiators, but never cram all three into one headline. Lead with one per concept; support with the others.
- **The mechanism is the magic.** "The people who already visited your site and left" is more believable than "we generate leads out of thin air." Anchor at least half the batch on the recovery-of-lost-traffic mechanism.
- **Compliance-safe framing.** Say "opt-in" / "consent-first" / "they agreed to hear from you." Never imply scraping, buying data, or contacting people who didn't consent. This is the whole brand.
- **Price is a wedge, not the point.** $7 flat, exclusive, vs. $80–150 shared leads on Angi/Thumbtack/Networx. Use the comparison, don't just shout "cheap."

## Image-generation calls (Recraft)

These are **specification calls** — no images are actually generated in this deliverable. Each is a Recraft request formatted for the client's pipeline. Rationale:

- **Format:** 1080×1080 (1:1) primary for Feed; each concept also lists a 1080×1350 (4:5) variant string since 4:5 wins reach in Feed. Reels/Stories (1080×1920) can be cropped from 4:5 or regenerated if the concept scales.
- **Style:** `digital_illustration` to stay on-brand with the Consent Resolve illustration system (matches the `recraft-batch` pipeline). Avoid `realistic_image` — brand is illustration-first, and stock-photo HVAC techs read as generic.
- **Text-in-image:** Recraft renders text unreliably, so prompts request **minimal or no baked-in text**; the $7 / "never resold" / headline lines live in the ad copy and any overlay is added in post. Where a concept benefits from a number, it's requested as a bold graphic element and flagged for post-production overlay as the safer path.
- **Brand color:** requests specify the Consent Resolve palette (deep navy/blue primary with a warm accent) so outputs drop into the HSL-recolor step of the existing pipeline.

## Launch / testing recommendation

1. **Ship all 5 concepts** into one ad set, let Meta's Advantage+ find the winners on Leads optimization (don't hand-throttle — you have enough distinct angles).
2. **Test Concept 1 (mechanism) and Concept 2 (never resold) hardest** — they carry the clearest differentiation and are least likely to be misread as a homeowner ad.
3. **Watch for a homeowner-audience mismatch signal:** if comments/leads come from people wanting AC repair, tighten targeting to owner/operator job titles and business-software interests (ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro).
4. **Two headlines per concept** are built in — run them as Meta's built-in text variations or as duplicate ads for cleaner attribution.
5. **Overlay all headline/number text in post-production**, not in Recraft. Concept 3 especially must not rely on Recraft-rendered numbers.
6. **Compliance gate:** before publishing, confirm every line reads as consent-first / opt-in and nothing implies buying or scraping data. Pull the "never resold" and "opted in" claims through the brand voice check.
